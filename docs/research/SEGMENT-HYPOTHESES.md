# Segment hypotheses (HYPOTHESES — not findings)

Each line is a belief of the builder, written so it can be proven wrong. None has been tested with real people.

| #   | Hypothesis                                                                                                                                                      | What would disprove it                                                                    |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| H1  | Founders setting up a new business want one place to see money set aside across workspace, equipment, contractors and services, more than they want automation. | Participants ignore the Launch board and Finance screens and ask only for "do it for me". |
| H2  | A small business will accept that an agent only proposes if each proposal shows who acts next and what is at risk.                                              | Participants consider "you must approve" a reason not to use it, even with the board.     |
| H3  | The words "Outcome unclear. Don't book again yet." stop duplicate bookings.                                                                                     | Participants book again anyway, or cannot say why they should wait.                       |
| H4  | People distinguish "Confirmed by the provider" from "Confirmed by you" and care about the difference.                                                           | Participants call both "confirmed" and are not bothered.                                  |
| H5  | Owners of two-person companies do not want an approval quorum; only companies with 3+ owners or an accountant do.                                               | Many two-owner companies turn the rule on.                                                |
| H6  | Recurring renewals are better handled as "ask me each time" than as automatic.                                                                                  | Participants say they would rather it renewed on its own within a limit.                  |

## Segments being considered (guesses, unsized)

Solo founders; two-to-five person agencies buying tools and contractor time; small event organisers booking spaces; small shops replenishing stock. No segment has been sized or interviewed. Nothing here is a market estimate.
