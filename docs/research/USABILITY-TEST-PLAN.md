# Usability test plan (PLAN ONLY — no sessions have been run)

**Not research evidence.** This is a plan. Participants have not been recruited and no result is recorded or implied.

## Questions the first round must answer

1. Can a person who has never seen PayFlow create an account, add an agent, describe a mission and approve it, without help?
2. Do people understand, from the screen alone, the difference between _held_, _committed_, _confirmed by the provider_, _confirmed by you_ and _outcome unclear_?
3. When a booking is "Outcome unclear", do they know not to book again and know what to do next?
4. Do they notice that PayFlow never buys by itself, and does that reassure or frustrate them?

## Participants (to be recruited with consent; none yet)

Five people who run or help run a small business and have bought equipment, a workspace or contractor work in the last year. At least two who have never used an AI assistant for purchases. Screen out anyone who works at a competitor or has seen PayFlow before. Record consent, compensation and the right to stop. Do not use friends of the team as the only participants.

## Tasks (think-aloud, 45 minutes, sandbox with fictional sources, stated at the start)

- T1 Sign up and add an agent. T2 Describe a mission to find a workspace and approve it. T3 Search, propose a booking, approve it, record that the provider confirmed it using only your own word. T4 A booking is shown as "Outcome unclear": say what you would do. T5 Read the Finance screen and say how much is at risk now. T6 Find out why something was skipped on a recurring item.

## Measures and what would count as a failure

Task completion without help; time on task; errors; the participant's own paraphrase of each state word (scored against the intended meaning); one question after T3: "Is the space reserved?" The intended answer is "No proof, only my word". Any participant who answers "yes, reserved" is a defect in the wording, not in the participant. Report all failures; do not exclude participants after the fact.

## Rules for honesty

The sandbox and its fictional providers are told to participants. Quotes are reported verbatim and attributed to a participant number only. Results are written up even if they are unflattering. Nothing from a session is claimed as adoption, demand or willingness to pay.

## Appendix: competitive review method (not performed)

Choose six products that a small business might use for purchasing, bookings or contractor work. For each, capture from public pages only: what an approval looks like, what a refund or dispute looks like, and what the product says it does with money. Record the URL and capture date for every statement. Do not infer features from marketing language.
