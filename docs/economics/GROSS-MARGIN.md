**HYPOTHETICAL - NOT VALIDATED. Every revenue figure below is HYPOTHETICAL.** PayFlow has no customers, no revenue and no validated price. The three tiers are invented usage shapes to show how the cost model behaves; they are not customers, forecasts or targets. Cost figures are model outputs from [COST-MODEL.md](COST-MODEL.md) (VERIFIED vendor prices plus ESTIMATE usage shapes) and are **lower bounds** because Vercel invocation pricing is UNVERIFIED.

This file is generated by `node --import tsx scripts/cost-model.ts --margin`. Do not hand-edit the tables; change the script and regenerate.

Gross margin = (revenue - cost) / revenue. Cost includes variable infrastructure, an allocated share of platform fixed cost, and the support-time ESTIMATE (2 hours x $75 per month at the default, 10x for the enterprise tier).

## Reading the result

1. **Infrastructure is not the constraint at these assumptions.** Variable infrastructure cost is $0.33 to $167 per month across tiers, against a support-time estimate of $150 to $1,500. The margin is determined mostly by how much human time an account consumes and by the (unvalidated) revenue figure.
2. **The small tier is negative** under the hypothetical $0.01 per action, because 2 hours of support exceeds $100 of revenue. That is an artifact of the support assumption and the hypothetical price, and it argues for self-serve, low-touch plans or a minimum fee. It is not a finding about the market.
3. **The dominant cost is support time** in all three tiers, so sensitivity is shown on the support hourly cost. At 10x the margin falls to about 31% for the enterprise tier and the mid-market tier drops to about 70%.
4. If measured infrastructure shape turns out to be orders of magnitude worse than the estimates, Neon storage and Neon baseline compute are the next-largest levers; re-run the script with scaled inputs (`scaleInput`).
5. Free SANDBOX and VERIFY usage carries cost with no revenue; it is not included in these tiers.

## What would change this

Measured per-operation cost on the live path; actual support hours; an actual price; vendor plan and region; Neon suspend behavior; production-rail costs; real retention requirements.
